## 🎭 **Role-Based Access**

The system supports three user roles. Select your role on the login page, then use **any valid email and password** (minimum 6 characters) to sign in.

### Demo Mode Instructions:
1. Go to the login page
2. Select a role from the dropdown (Customer/Technician/Admin)
3. **Option A:** Enter **any email address** (e.g., test@example.com) and **any password** (min 6 chars, e.g., demo123)
4. **Option B:** Click "Sign in with Google" (simulates OAuth login)
5. You'll be automatically logged in!
